Privacy & storage

Your entries are stored in this browser on this device. No bank connection or account is required. Clearing website data can erase your budget, so keep backups.

Install PocketBudget

On iPhone, open the published site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. If available, turn on “Open as Web App.” For offline use, open the site at least once while online so its app files can be cached.

Important: scheduled paychecks are processed when you open the app; the browser does not run this app in the background while it is closed. Pay calculations are estimates and do not include tax, overtime, or employer payroll rules.
